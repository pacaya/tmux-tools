### H1. The live check that the Leader's row does not report the teammate's status cannot fail (FIXED)
> **Fix:** Teammate prompt drives an identifiable transition; `judge_teammate_transition` requires the teammate row's `mainAgent` to advance and the Leader row's `mainAgent` to stay unchanged, including at turn end; unit test with mutation check; live pass.

Source: CLAUDE-1, CODEX-1 (both agree). `tests/contract/orca.rs:2408-2409`.

BLOCKER: Acceptance item 4 ("in `orca worktree ps --json`, the agent row whose `paneKey` is the teammate pane's key reports the teammate's status, and the Leader's row does not") and PRD-261108-0447-01 P10.6 ("Orca shows the teammate's status on its own pane, not on the Leader pane"). `agent_row(&ps, &lead_key)` selects the row by `paneKey == lead_key`, and the test then asserts that row's `paneKey` equals `lead_key`. This can never fail. The teammate half checks only a non-null state. Both pass even if the teammate's main-agent status is attributed to the Leader.

Required outcome: a falsifiable live check. Drive a teammate state transition that can be identified independently, for example a `prompt` into `<lead>.tm1` (allowed by P8.4) after the Leader's own turn has settled. Then require that the transition shows in the teammate row's own `mainAgent` (`state` and/or `stateStartedAt` advance), and that the Leader row's `mainAgent` does not take it (its `stateStartedAt` is unchanged by the teammate transition). The Leader row's `subagents[]` entry for the teammate is allowed (P8.5, Notes item 9). Do not count that entry as the teammate's status, and do not compare only the two aggregate `host_state` values. If the live Orca answers cannot separate the two rows by any such observation, stop and report `PREMISE_CHANGED` with the evidence, and do not weaken the assertion.
