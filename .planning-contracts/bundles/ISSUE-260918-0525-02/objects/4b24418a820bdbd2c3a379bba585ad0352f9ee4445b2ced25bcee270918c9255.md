## Agent Brief

**Outcome and scope:** The agent registry models surfaces, `spawn-agent` selects and records one,
readiness reads the recorded surface, and pane state is one pure three-valued classifier. Every
agent's default surface stays what it is today.

**Required sources:** `tmux-tools:PRD-260918-0323-01#p2-surface` (all of it except promoting the
flat surface to default, which `ISSUE-260918-0525-07` owns);
`tmux-tools:PRD-260918-0323-01#p3-state` (the classifier and its truth table).

**Current observable:** `ready_regex`/`ready_lines` belong to the agent, not a rendering. Nothing on
a pane records which rendering it was launched with, and `spawn-agent` cannot choose one.

**Required change:**
- P2's surface map on `AgentSpec`, with every field P2 lists. `ready_regex`/`ready_lines` move onto
  the surface. Built-ins: `claude` gets a rich surface (today's launch) and a flat one
  (`--ax-screen-reader`); `codex`, `cursor` and `agy` each get one surface, their current rendering.
  Each agent's pre-surface-rendering surface is its default. Flat surfaces carry no validated
  patterns yet; `ISSUE-260918-0525-07` supplies them.
- On built-in surfaces the remaining fields ship values that preserve today's behavior: no
  bracketed-paste capability, interrupt key `C-c`, no quit hazard, no resume syntax. Real values
  come from `ISSUE-260918-0525-03` (paste capability), `-05` (resume) and `-07` (quit hazard and
  measured patterns).
- README documents how to declare `dsh`'s surfaces in `agents.toml`: rich `--profile tui`, flat
  `--profile dshline`. `dsh` stays out of the built-ins.
- `spawn-agent --surface <name>`; no surface flag on `launch`.
- Unconditional recording of the resolved surface in pane registration; total resolution (record →
  that surface, no record → the pre-surface-rendering surface, no agent → none); the surface-unvalidated mark for
  caller-supplied trailing arguments. Arguments the surface supplies itself do not set the mark.
- A pre-surface `agents.toml` still loads, and its readiness fields bind to the surface matching the
  agent's pre-surface rendering. An agent declared without surfaces loads as one surface built from
  its agent-level readiness fields; that surface is its pre-surface-rendering surface and its
  default.
  Exactly one surface per agent is the default, and that still holds after the `agents.toml`
  deep-merge.
- P3's classifier in `core/src/idle.rs` as the only place pane state is derived. Readiness consumers
  (`wait-idle`, `prompt`) read the resolved surface's patterns.
  When the resolved surface supplies no patterns, or there is no surface, they use idle detection.
- Extend `tt-fake-tui` to render a configurable ready/busy footer.
- Record the surface model as an ADR. Mint this PRD's frontmatter terms in CONTEXT.md, stating the
  narrowing of **Readiness** and **Idle detection** P2 requires.

**Acceptance:** Items 1 and 3 use an `agents.toml` agent whose default surface is not its
pre-surface-rendering surface, with surfaces rendering distinguishable fixture ready footers.
- [ ] Given a pane `spawn-agent` created with no surface named, when `wait-idle` runs against it,
  then it settles on the default surface's ready footer and not the pre-surface one's, observable at
  the CLI.
- [ ] Given a pane spawned with `--surface <name>`, when its registration is read, then it records
  that surface and is not marked surface-unvalidated even when that surface supplies launch
  arguments; given one spawned with trailing caller arguments, it is marked surface-unvalidated.
  Observable in tmux pane options.
- [ ] Given a pane carrying an agent but no surface record, when `wait-idle` runs, then it settles on
  the pre-surface-rendering surface's ready footer and not the default's, observable at the CLI.
- [ ] Given a pre-surface-shape `agents.toml` overriding a built-in agent's readiness fields, then
  the file loads, the overrides govern a pane on that agent's pre-surface-rendering surface, and
  they do not govern a pane spawned with `--surface` on its other surface, observable through
  `wait-idle` against the fixture.
- [ ] Given a pre-surface-shape `agents.toml` declaring a custom agent with no surfaces, then it
  loads and its readiness fields govern `wait-idle` on a pane of that agent.
- [ ] Given an `agents.toml` naming a different surface of a built-in agent as its default, when
  `spawn-agent` runs with no surface named, then that surface is recorded, and the agent has
  exactly one default, observable in pane registration.
- [ ] Given a pane on a surface with no patterns, and a `launch` pane, when `wait-idle` runs, then
  each settles by idle detection, observable at the CLI.
- [ ] Given the built-in registry, then every agent has exactly one default surface, and `codex`,
  `cursor` and `agy` resolve to their single surface carrying today's readiness fields, observable
  through `wait-idle` behaving as before on a pane of each.
- [ ] Each row of P3's truth table returns its stated result, observable at the classifier's public
  function.

**Verification and release:** Truth-table unit tests on the classifier; integration tests at the
CLI seam with `tt-fake-tui`; full workspace suite green under the documented test command.

**Out of scope:** Promoting any flat surface to default; interrupt, resume, transport, capture and
prompt-output behavior (sibling packets); detecting that an agent has exited; additional surfaces for
`cursor` or `agy`; making `dsh` a built-in.
